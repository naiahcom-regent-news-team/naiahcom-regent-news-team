We Are DAZ-i

The Naiahcom High School Regent Press Team is a student-led journalism club dedicated to informing, inspiring, and connecting our school community. From breaking campus news to in-depth international analysis, we cover every angle — with curiosity, creativity, and integrity.

🎯

Our Mission

To foster a culture of informed, critical thinking through student-driven journalism that elevates every voice on campus.

📡

What We Do

We publish news stories, run broadcasts, produce video content, write editorials, and cover sports and culture throughout the school year.

🌱

Who We Are

Students from SSS1–SSS3 across Arts, Science, and Commercial streams — united by a love for storytelling and public service.

🏆

Our Advisor

Mr. Bomah guides us with wisdom and warmth, creating a safe space to experiment, fail, learn, and grow as journalists.
